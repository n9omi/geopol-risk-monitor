# Calculation reference

_Generated by `tests/check_calcs.mjs` from the titles and descriptions in `site/calcs.js`, in execution order._

## `universe` — Series universe: core markets, desk data and derived spreads

Inputs: `markets`, `desk`, `fed_daily`

All daily series in one table with their desk and change convention (price = log %, rate = basis points, level = absolute), including the 2-year Treasury par yield. Derived daily: 3-2-1 crack = (2 × RBOB × 42 + ULSD × 42 − 3 × WTI) ÷ 3; diesel crack = ULSD × 42 − WTI; Brent–WTI; VIX ÷ VIX 3-month; 3m10y = 10-year − 3-month yield; 5s30s = 30-year − 5-year; CCC–BBB OAS; ITA ÷ JETS; XLE and SMH relative to the S&P 500 (×1000).

## `latest` — Latest readings, changes and momentum

Inputs: `universe`

Per series: last close; changes over 5 and 21 trading days and since 27 Feb 2026 (last close before the Iran war), in log % for prices, bp for yields and spreads, absolute for levels (cracks, ratios); also simple % changes; percentile rank of the last close within its window (one year for core series, since 3 Nov 2025 for desk series); 20-day momentum z = 20-day change ÷ (daily st. dev. × √20); 5-day z likewise.

## `stress` — Stress composite and pricing gap

Inputs: `markets`

Each day all five have a close: one-year percentile ranks (0 = year low, 100 = year high) of Brent, 10-year yield, MOVE, VIX and HY OAS. Composite = average of all five. Pricing gap = average of the shock channels (Brent, 10-year, MOVE) minus the VIX percentile (how much of the shock equity volatility prices).

## `stress_latest` — Regime read, latest

Inputs: `stress`

Latest stress composite and pricing gap, their change over 20 trading days, the gap's percentile within its own history, and rule-based labels: composite ≥ 70 Stressed, ≥ 45 Elevated, else Calm; gap ≥ 40 equity vol cheap vs the shock, ≤ −10 equity vol rich, else aligned.

## `gap_backtest` — Pricing-gap backtest (in-sample)

Inputs: `stress`, `markets`

For every day with a stress reading: VIX change in points and S&P 500 log % return over the next 10 trading days, grouped by the pricing gap that day (wide ≥ 50, moderate 25–49, narrow < 25). Overlapping windows within one year of data — evidence, not proof.

## `oil_beta` — Oil-shock beta

Inputs: `universe`

Beta and correlation of each series' daily move (log % for prices, bp for yields and spreads, absolute for levels) to Brent's daily log % move, over the last 60 shared days and over the war period (from 2 Mar 2026).

## `event_moves` — Event study: moves after 2026 Middle East events

Inputs: `universe`, `catalysts`

For each dated 2026 Middle East escalation (+1) or de-escalation (−1) event: the move from the last close before the event to the 5th close on or after it (log % for prices, bp for yields and spreads, absolute for levels).

## `event_study` — Event study: average 2026 reaction by series

Inputs: `event_moves`

Per series: mean 5-day move after 2026 escalation and de-escalation events, number of events, share of events in which the series rose, and sensitivity = escalation mean − de-escalation mean.

## `hist_moves` — Historical analogs: moves after 2019–2025 events

Inputs: `hist`, `hist_events`

Same 5-day method applied to eight earlier geopolitical shocks (Abqaiq 2019, Soleimani and the stand-down 2020, Ukraine 2022, 7 October 2023, Iran–Israel April 2024, June 2025 strikes and ceasefire) for Brent, VIX, S&P 500, gold, the 10-year yield and the dollar index.

## `analogs` — History vs 2026: escalation response

Inputs: `hist_moves`, `event_moves`

Per series with history: average 5-day escalation move and share of rises in 2019–2025 analogs, in 2026 events, and pooled; "Agree" when both samples move the same way on average.

## `hedge_rank` — Hedge efficacy in escalations

Inputs: `event_study`, `latest`

Per series: average 5-day move after 2026 Middle East escalations divided by its typical 5-day move (daily st. dev. × √5) — a vol-adjusted escalation z-score; positive means the series rises when the conflict escalates. Ranked from strongest riser to strongest faller.

## `scenario_moves` — Scenario-weighted expected move

Inputs: `event_study`, `scenarios`

Per series: probability-weighted 5-day move = P(escalation) × average escalation move + P(de-escalation) × average de-escalation move (status quo contributes zero). Probabilities are analyst judgments.

## `exposure` — Geopolitical exposure index

Inputs: `framework`, `catalysts`

Per asset class: sum over live catalysts of (analyst risk score 1–5 × the asset's exposure rating 0–3 to that catalyst's channel), scaled so the most exposed asset = 100. Also the channel contributing most.

## `signals` — Signal board

Inputs: `framework`, `exposure`, `latest`, `oil_beta`, `scenario_moves`, `event_study`, `analogs`

Per asset class: priced = the gauge's one-year percentile (inverted where a high reading means complacency, e.g. TAIEX); gap = exposure index − priced. Confirmation: the gauge moved in its risk-priced direction on average and in at least half of 2026 escalations; where 2019–2025 history exists it must also do so there ("Mixed" if only one sample agrees). Gap ≥ +25 and confirmed = Under-priced (hedge); gap ≥ +25 otherwise = Watch; gap ≤ −25 = Rich (fade the premium); else Fair. Conviction High if |gap| ≥ 50, Medium if ≥ 25. Vehicle: where an implied-vol index exists (OVX, GVZ, MOVE), options are preferred when implied vol sits ≥ 25 percentile points below the spot gauge, outright when ≥ 25 above.

## `trade_ideas` — Trade ideas, ranked

Inputs: `signals`

Actionable signals ordered for a morning meeting: Under-priced first, then Rich, then Watch; within each by the size of the gap. Top six.

## `movers` — What moved this week

Inputs: `latest`

The ten largest 5-day moves across all series, ranked by size relative to each series' normal 5-day move (5-day change ÷ (daily st. dev. × √5)).

## `upcoming` — Next 30 days

Inputs: `calendar`, `markets`

Calendar entries dated after the last market close and within 31 days of it, with days to go.

## `desk_view` — Desk reads

Inputs: `latest`, `signals`, `desks`, `fred_latest`, `hedge_rank`

For each desk, a rule-based read assembled from its metrics (percentile ranks, changes since 27 Feb and over a month, implied vs spot), plus its signal count and strongest flag. Rules are written out in the Method tab.

## `roadmap_stats` — Deliverables headway

Inputs: `roadmap`

Count of deliverables by status, and the share live (including proxy versions).

## `region_summary` — Live catalysts by region

Inputs: `catalysts`

Live (non-historical) catalysts per region: count, number escalating, highest analyst risk score and the top-ranked catalyst.

## `fred_latest` — FRED latest observations

Inputs: `fred`

Per FRED series: the latest observation and its change in basis points since the first observation shown.

## `premium` — Dated Brent premium to futures

Inputs: `fred`, `markets`

FRED dated Brent spot (DCOILBRENTEU) minus the Brent futures close on the same date.

## `premium_latest` — Dated Brent premium, latest

Inputs: `premium`

The most recent day with both a dated Brent and a futures close.

## `brent_wti` — Brent–WTI spread (physical snapshot)

Inputs: `indicators`

Brent minus WTI from the same Straits brief snapshot.

## `hormuz_ratio` — Hormuz transits vs normal

Inputs: `indicators`

Daily transits as a share of the pre-crisis baseline.

## `gpr_stats` — Daily GPR, period average

Inputs: `gpr`

Mean and maximum of the daily GPR values shown.

## `odds_shift` — Midterm odds, March vs October

Inputs: `predictions`

Average Democratic-control probability across Kalshi and Polymarket for House and Senate, at each snapshot date.

## `analog_events` — Historical analogs by event

Inputs: `hist_moves`, `hist_events`

The 5-day moves from Historical analogs laid out one row per event (log % for prices, bp for the 10-year yield).

## `fed_rates` — Fed rates corridor, daily

Inputs: `fed_daily`

One row per business day from 6 Nov 2025: EFFR, SOFR and their 99th percentiles, the target range, IORB (top of range − 10 bp), ON RRP take-up and the 3-month, 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields. Spreads in bp: EFFR − IORB, SOFR − IORB, SOFR 99th percentile − standing repo rate (top of range), 2-year − EFFR, 3-month − EFFR and 2s10s.

## `fed_week` — Fed balance sheet, weekly (H.4.1)

Inputs: `fed_weekly`

Wednesday levels in $bn: total assets, reserve balances, Treasury General Account, Treasury and MBS holdings, repo (standing repo facility) and primary credit (discount window).

## `fed_now` — Fed dashboard, latest

Inputs: `fed_rates`, `fed_week`, `fed_comms`, `fed_decisions`, `fed_pricing`

Latest corridor readings with 5-day averages and 20-day extremes; weekly balance-sheet levels with 4-week changes (6-week for the discount window) and the 13-week high in standing repo use; days to the next FOMC decision (28 Oct 2026); latest market-implied hike odds per meeting; Fedspeak tone = average stance score (−2 dovish to +2 hawkish) of communications in the 30 days to the last market date; the last decision and its vote.

## `fed_monitor` — Fed plumbing monitor

Inputs: `fed_now`, `fed_playbook`

Each micro-indicator's latest reading against analyst thresholds in the playbook: Stress beyond the alert level, Watch beyond the warning level, otherwise Normal (Info where no threshold applies). Readings: EFFR − IORB latest; SOFR − IORB and SOFR 99th percentile − standing repo rate as 5-day averages; ON RRP 5-day average; standing repo, reserves, discount window at the latest Wednesday; TGA and Treasury holdings as 4-week changes.

## `fed_read` — Fed read

Inputs: `fed_now`, `fed_monitor`

Rule-based summary: policy stance from the last decision, Fedspeak tone and market pricing; plumbing status from the monitor (Stress if any indicator is in stress, Watch if two or more are on watch, else Ample).

## `fed_betas` — Rate-shock betas and FOMC-day moves

Inputs: `universe`, `fed_daily`, `fed_decisions`, `fed_comms`

For each asset: the beta of its daily move (log % for prices, bp for yields and spreads) to the daily change in the 2-year Treasury yield holding Brent's daily move constant (two-factor regression on all shared days since Oct 2025), shown per +10 bp in the 2-year. Controlling for oil strips out the war's inflation channel, so the beta approximates a monetary-policy shock. Cross-check: a simple beta on Fed event days only (FOMC decisions plus the dated communications in the tracker), and whether its sign agrees. Also the simple correlation with the 2-year and the average absolute move on FOMC decision days.

## `fomc_reactions` — FOMC decisions and market reaction

Inputs: `fed_decisions`, `universe`, `fed_daily`

Each decision since October 2025 with its vote and dissents (hawkish = wanted tighter policy, dovish = wanted easier), and the one-day move on decision day (close to close): 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields in bp; S&P 500, dollar index, gold, VIX and USD/JPY in log %.

## `fed_breakdown` — FOMC scenario × asset breakdown

Inputs: `fed_scenarios`, `fed_betas`

For each scenario on 28 Oct: the assumed 2-year yield shock (analyst) × each asset's rate-shock beta = expected same-day move (bp for yields, spreads and curve; % for prices). Expected = probability-weighted across the four scenarios. Betas are one-year oil-controlled averages; "agree" shows whether Fed-event days point the same way. They will understate moves if the Fed surprises on the balance sheet or forward guidance.

## `shock_betas` — Shock factor betas

Inputs: `universe`

For every series, sensitivities to three ordered shocks over the last 250 shared days: (1) the 2-year Treasury yield, per bp, and (2) Brent, per 1%, from a regression on both together, so a Fed shock carries its usual equity and FX reaction and an oil shock is measured with rates held constant; (3) an equity shock, the S&P 500 coefficient per 1% from adding it as a third regressor, i.e. a sell-off beyond what rates and oil explain. Also R² of the three-factor fit and the series' daily standard deviation.

## `shock_matrix` — Shock × asset impacts

Inputs: `shocks`, `shock_betas`, `event_study`, `analogs`

Every series under each defined shock. Event shocks use measured average moves: 5-day moves after 2026 Hormuz escalations or de-escalations, or after the 2019–25 analogs (six series only). Factor shocks use the shock betas: move = 2-year shock × 2-year beta + Brent shock × oil beta + extra equity shock × equity beta (same-day). z = move ÷ (daily st. dev. × √horizon). Position z = the z a long holder earns: the same as z for prices, levels and FX, and reversed for yields and spreads, since a long bond or credit position gains when they fall.

## `shock_hedges` — Hedges and exposures by shock

Inputs: `shock_matrix`, `analogs`

For each shock, up to five series whose long position gains most (best hedges, position z above zero) and up to five that lose most (most exposed, position z below zero), so no series appears on both sides when a shock covers only a few series. Curve spreads and ratio series are left out because a "long" position in them is ambiguous; product cracks stay in (long crack = long products, short crude). For the geopolitical shocks, also flags series whose 2026 response disagrees with the 2019–25 analogs.

## `shock_transmission` — Transmission by desk

Inputs: `shock_matrix`

For each shock and desk: the average absolute z across the desk's series and the series that moves most, showing where a shock lands first.

## `combo_scenarios` — Combined scenarios: Hormuz × October FOMC

Inputs: `scenarios`, `fed_scenarios`, `event_study`, `shock_betas`

Every pairing of the three Hormuz scenarios with the four FOMC outcomes. Probability = product of the two, assuming independence. Move = the Hormuz bundle (average 5-day escalation or de-escalation move; zero for the status quo) + the Fed outcome's 2-year shock × each series' 2-year beta. 60/40 = 0.6 × S&P 500 move + 0.4 × the 10-year price change (≈ −8 × yield change in %). Expected = probability-weighted across the twelve. A stress test (twice the average escalation plus a surprise hike) is shown without a probability.

## `combo_summary` — Scenario stress summary

Inputs: `combo_scenarios`

Across the twelve Hormuz × FOMC pairings: the worst and best 60/40 outcome among scenarios with at least 5% probability, the probability-weighted 60/40 move, and the stress-test 60/40 move.
